## Findings (Minor, non-blocking, backlog)

- **m1 (copy):** Lineup's summary banner always reads "...projected {delta} pts" even in
  Safe/Upside mode, contradicting the active mode tab. Make the wording mode-aware or drop
  "projected". File: `lineup/_components/format.ts` (`lineupSummary`).
- **m2 (rounding/threshold):** The banner's total and the swap rows below it round
  independently and can disagree (e.g. banner "-0.0 pts" while rows sum to "+0.1 pts"); the
  banner also has no floor matching Home's `hasSwaps` >= 0.05 pt guard, so it can headline
  "N swaps available" for a net-zero or negative rearrangement. Apply the same threshold and
  avoid ever displaying a signed "-0.0". Files: `lineup/_components/summary-banner.tsx`,
  `format.ts`.
- **m3 (consistency):** "This is your team" is marked two different ways on the same Home page:
  `Badge variant="accent"` on the team card vs. `Badge variant="you"` (vivid purple pill) on the
  standings row, League, and Lineup header. Standardize on one, recommended `variant="you"`.
  Files: `app/l/[leagueId]/(main)/page.tsx`, `app/l/[leagueId]/_components/team-view.tsx`.
