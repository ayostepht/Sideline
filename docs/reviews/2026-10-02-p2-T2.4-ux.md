## Works well

- "You" treatment and position badges are consistent across Home, League and team detail.
- Injury badges pair an icon, letter and detail text.
- Dark mode keeps contrast; spacing and radii are consistent.
- No horizontal scroll at 390; table at desktop and cards at mobile; tabular numbers.
- Onboarding source: aria-live, role="alert", sr-only legend, 44 px targets, motion-safe spinners, plain copy.
- No em dashes found.
