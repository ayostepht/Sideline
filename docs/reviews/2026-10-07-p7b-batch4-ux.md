# UX review: Phase 7b Batch 4 (Lineup Auto, Trades, Home)

Date: 2026-10-07 | Commit: `8fc0ddb` | Reviewer: ux-reviewer
VERDICT: CHANGES REQUIRED (3 Major). Screens: `.screens/l-1000000000000000001-{lineup,trades,trades__tab-analyzer}/`, `.screens/ux4/` (not committed).

## Major
- **M1** Trades Finder, all widths: ranking and badges contradict. Top pick carries "Lopsided"; 9 of 10 cards are Lopsided (e.g. give two WRs for a DEF, their lineup +60.7 vs mine +1.5, my playoffs 96% to 95%). The lime "Top pick" recommends a trade the app itself flags as bad. Fix: ranking and filtering in the finder (data), and never a Top pick on a Lopsided trade (UI). See ADR-022 amendment (item 7a).
- **M2** Trades and Lineup at 390 and 1280: fairness and Auto explanations are hover/`title` only, so touch users can't learn them; info icons are under 44px. Fix: tap-to-open popover, a visible one-line Auto hint, 44px targets.
- **M3** Trades Finder cold load: `pnpm screens` timed out at 30 s networkidle at 390 and 768 (warm 1280 passed). Possibly contention with the host dev server on port 3000. Fix: measure the cold path on the fixture DB; stream the Finder behind Suspense with a card skeleton; cache or precompute if slow.

## Minor
- **m1** Analyzer before/after is plain text ("2405.8 to 2407.3 pts"); use an aligned before/after grid with `tabular-nums` and down arrows for drops (Finder playoff drop too).
- **m2** Analyzer at 390: nested scroll lists hide selected players; show selections as chips or sort them first; no nested scroll on mobile.
- **m3** Finder cards: repeated "With Team 03", two stacked text links, sparse cards at 1280. Group actions on one row, "Open in Analyzer" as a 44px button, cap width or two columns at 1280.
- **m4** Lineup toggle segments about 40px high; use `min-h-11`.

## Nit
- **n1** WEATHER chips not visibly reviewed; covered by P7b.11's review.
