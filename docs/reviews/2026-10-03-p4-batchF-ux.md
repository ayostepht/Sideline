# Phase 4 Batch F UX review

Date: 2026-10-03. Branch: `phase/4-waivers`. Route: `/l/{leagueId}` (Home) at 390/768/1280px, light/dark. Reviewer: ux-reviewer subagent.

VERDICT: CHANGES REQUIRED (2 Major; fixed before Batch G)

## Findings

**[M1] Major** Home's "Waiver targets" card shows three candidates all with negative Lineup Impact (picking any of them up would make the lineup worse) with no caveat, presenting them as recommendations. This directly contradicts the Waivers page's own established pattern: `apps/web/app/l/[leagueId]/waivers/_components/format.ts`'s `topTargetSummary` deliberately suppresses its headline when `lineupImpact <= 0`, precisely to avoid claiming a non-positive move is a recommendation. Home's `topWaiverTargets` just slices the top 3 regardless of sign. **Fix:** apply the same non-positive guard — when every row's `lineupImpact <= 0`, fall back to an `EmptyState` ("No upgrades beat your roster this week") or add an inline qualifier.

**[M2] Major** Home's "Rising players" card has no "See all"/next-action link, unlike every other Home card (Team, Standings, Waiver targets all end with one). This breaks PLAN 6.5's requirement that an empty state pair its message with a next action, and breaks "same component, same job everywhere" consistency. In the populated case there's also no way to see more than the capped 5 risers. **Fix:** add a `Link` to `${base}/players` in both the populated and empty branches, styled identically to the sibling "See all" links.

## Minor / Nit (backlog, not blocking)

- **[m1]** Waiver-target rows are static (no link/hover/chevron) while riser rows are fully clickable links — each internally consistent, but a user who taps into a riser row may expect the same from a waiver-target row just above it. Matches the brief's literal per-card spec; leaving as a documented, deliberate asymmetry is acceptable, or a future pass could link waiver-target rows to player detail too.
- **[n1]** A known full-page-screenshot capture artifact (duplicated fixed bottom-nav) lands across the Waiver targets card at 390/768px — confirmed not a real rendering issue against the 1280px desktop capture.

## What works well

- Both new cards reuse the exact Standings-card chrome (`sl-label sl-mark` header, `divide-y` rows, `min-h-11` touch targets, `tabular-nums` values) — a strong template for future Home cards.
- "Nobody is trending up" empty state reads as informative and calm, not broken.
- No new accent color introduced; Home's one lime accent stays on the lineup-insight CTA.
- Position badges, signed-point formatting, and touch targets are pixel-identical to the Waivers page's own rows.

## Accessibility

Zero axe violations (6/6, all projects/themes). No horizontal scroll at 390px (3/3 projects).
